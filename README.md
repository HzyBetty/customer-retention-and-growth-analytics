# Retail Customer Growth Strategy Analysis

Business analytics project focused on customer retention, CRM strategy, and KPI-driven growth planning for a Quebec craft distillery facing market saturation and declining retail visibility.

**Focus Areas:**  
Customer Analytics | CRM Strategy | Retail Growth | KPI Evaluation | Forecasting | Business Analytics

## Business Problem

The Quebec craft distillery industry experienced increasing market saturation, slowing growth, rising production costs, and limited retail shelf space. The business faced additional challenges in customer conversion and long-term retention despite strong tourism potential.

Key problems identified:
- Increasing competition with slowing market growth
- Heavy dependence on traditional retail channels
- Low tourist conversion and repeat visitation rates
- Limited CRM and customer retention capabilities

Key analytical areas included:

- Retention KPI monitoring

## KPI Evaluation & Forecasted Business Impact

The strategy projected measurable improvements in customer engagement, visitor conversion, and long-term profitability through CRM and experiential growth initiatives.

Projected outcomes included:
- $400K+ projected revenue growth
- 3× projected EBIT increase
- 158% estimated ROI over 6 years
- Improved customer conversion and retention metrics
